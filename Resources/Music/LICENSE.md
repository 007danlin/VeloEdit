# Bundled music

The 12 audio tracks in this directory are original recordings by
**HoliznaCC0**, downloaded from each track's Free Music Archive page.

Every track is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Attribution is not required. VeloEdit still keeps the artist, source page,
license URL, and license verification date in `manifest.json` and in project
music credits for provenance.

The audio files may be used, modified, copied, and redistributed, including
commercially, to the extent permitted by CC0 and applicable law. Free Music
Archive's site terms do not replace the individual CC0 dedication recorded on
each source page.
